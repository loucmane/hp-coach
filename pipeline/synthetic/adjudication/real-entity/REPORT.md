## Why the existing gates missed this

M-PLAGIARISM compares against the **authentic UHR corpus**; M-ECHO against **our own shipped units**.
Neither knows anything about the real world. G-REGISTER judges authenticity of *register*, not of
*reference*. The language and pedagogy reviews read the unit, not the world. Only an adversarial
auditor with web access ever caught one — and only by chance, twice. Hence law 16 and the standing
audit step.
