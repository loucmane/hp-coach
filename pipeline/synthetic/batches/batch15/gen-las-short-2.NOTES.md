One further deconfliction against `offentliga-ur-essa`: that unit's thesis is
"the public clock was a shared *agreement about a minute*, and agreements decay
when nobody needs them". A name-day essay built on "the almanac made the date
public knowledge, so congratulating needed no intimacy" would have been a
**thesis clone** of it. That framing was therefore dropped and replaced with the
**gift-ceiling** mechanism (see §2), which is an economics-of-obligation
argument, not a coordination-device argument.

## 2. Thesis and architecture — deliberately not the shipped essä mould

I read the two most recent shipped essäs in full (`las-b13-002` tatuering,
`las-b9-003` offentliga ur). They share one skeleton:

> [common metric dismissed as blunt] → [named researcher + inventory/archive
> count + proportions] → [uneven distribution] → [caveat paragraph: "Bilden är
> inte entydig" / "Jämförelsen är inte invändningsfri"] → [colleague's second
> study, then downgraded] → [concrete vignette object] → [aphoristic "inte X,
> utan Y" chiasmus close] → byline + glossary

That is a law-12 clone mould, and both instances also run the
"the metric measures the wrong thing" thesis that law 12 caps at ~1/batch, and
close on the chiasmus law 12 retires. This unit avoids all of it:

| axis | shipped essä mould | this unit |
|---|---|---|
| opening move | dismissed metric / anomaly | **definitional** — plainly states what a name-day *was* as a transaction |
| evidence base | inventory with counts and percentages (ledger-style, law-12 saturated) | one etiquette-book quotation + a scholar's reading of magazine advice columns; **no counts, no percentages, no ledgers** (the passage says so out loud: "spaltsvar är ett skevt material") |
| skeptic slot | a colleague/other researchers push back | **no skeptic** — the essayist herself grants part of the conventional explanation and then scopes it |
| thesis shape | metric-measures-the-wrong-thing | **mechanism-is-the-point** (the ceiling is what made wide, cheap attention possible) |
| caveat | dedicated "bilden är inte entydig" paragraph | hedging distributed inline (*brukar*, *förstås*, *varsamt*) |
| close | aphorism / "inte X, utan Y" | **simply stops** on a concrete particular (mormor's list, seven calls in May, five to people never invited home) |
| institution | "Institutionen för X i Y" (used by b4-002, b13-002, reference unit) | **none named** |
| toponyms | every shipped unit names a town | **zero toponyms** |
| researcher gender | careful-woman lead (18/18 in the bank scan) | **male lead scholar, female essayist** |

Phrase blocklist (law 14) checked line by line — no member and no close variant
present. In particular "Entydig är bilden inte", "Vi ser ett mönster, inte en
lag", "Metoden/Materialet har sina hål" and "Invändningen kommer genast" are all
absent; b9-003's "Den räcker inte." was deliberately paraphrased away
("Men det förklarar inte varför seden dröjde kvar…").

## 3. Planted targets

- **P2 — the ceiling (hedged, scoped, non-legal):** the 1934 etiquette book caps
  the gift at what the recipient can return without trouble; fifty öre bought
  the bouquet; anything dearer embarrassed the recipient; *"Taket var ingen
  regel, men det höll."*
- **P3 — the scope (directional, explicitly asymmetric):** the reach ran
  *outward* (neighbour, workmate, shop assistant), and the day meant **least**
  inside the household, where the birthday already did that job.
- **P4 — the direction (the invertible arrow):** the passage states the common
  assumption (*gift small because the day was already trivial*) and has Rimhall
  reverse it (*the day could stay small because the ceiling held*). This is the
  single most important planting: it hands Q1 its reversed-causality distractor
  pre-labelled as the thing the text rejects.
- **P5 — the conceded alternative, scoped:** the almanac's retreat "spelade
  förstås roll", but does not explain the decades of survival after it stopped
  being everyday reading. This is what makes Q2's overgeneralisation distractor
  wrong on *degree*, not on subject.
- **P6 — the change:** the ceiling stopped being self-evident, so even the
  smallest gesture became a choice, and choices force you to rank your
  acquaintances. The birthday is contrasted here (private date, no ceiling,
  gift read as a measure of closeness).

Deliberately **absent** from the passage: any mention of church, saints, or a
religious calendar — that absence is what makes Q2/D a clean
`plausible_worldknowledge` trap rather than an arguable reading.

## 4. Question design

### Q1 — `enligt_texten_detalj` (short-breath question, addendum §6)
Stem 8 words; options 6–8 words; key **D** (7 words, not the longest).

| opt | trap | why it tempts / why it fails |
|---|---|---|
| A | `reversed_causality` | the arrow the text names and inverts; a solver who reads P4 fast keeps the *common assumption* instead of Rimhall's reversal |
| B | `plausible_worldknowledge` | homemade gifts are period-plausible and would also explain cheapness — but the passage has everything bought (bouquet from the shopkeeper, cake, card) and says most people *spent* fifty öre |
| C | `scope_shift` | echoes the household sentence from P3 while inverting its point; the household is where the day mattered *least* |
| **D** | **key** | paraphrases P2 without lifting it (passage: "bör inte överstiga vad mottagaren utan besvär kan ge igen" / "Taket var ingen regel, men det höll" → option: "seden satte ett tak för gåvan") |

### Q2 — `huvudbudskap_syfte`
Corpus-attested stem verbatim in form only ("Vilket påstående överensstämmer
bäst med texten?" — ~6 tokens, far under the 17-token plagiarism kill length).
Key **B** (15 words); D is longer (16), so the key is not the length tell.

| opt | trap | identifiable flaw (law 11 — no verbatim-true distractor) |
|---|---|---|
| A | `overgeneralisation` | "helt och hållet i samma stund" — the passage concedes the almanac mattered but states the custom lingered *for decades* afterwards |
| **B** | **key** | spans P2 (ceiling) + P3 (reach) + P4 (direction) + P6 (ceiling gone → choice); no single paragraph carries it |
| C | `detail_as_main` + distortion | the etiquette book is real in the text but its advice concerns the **size of the gift**, not the **circle of recipients** — and the circle was wide, not family-only. Distorted, so it is not fully-true |
| D | `plausible_worldknowledge` | the saints'-calendar association is the first thing general knowledge offers; the passage contains no religious content at all |

### Key spread and the hedge–key correlation (law 10)
Keys land on **D** and **B** — no A anywhere, which is the direct counter to
batch14's ~16/18 A-bias (addendum §1). The hedge correlation is broken in both
directions: on **Q1 the key is the flattest, most confident option** and the
*hedge* ("för det mesta") sits on a distractor B; on **Q2 the absolutes sit on
distractor A** ("helt och hållet") and on **D** ("framför allt"), so "pick the
qualified option" does not locate the key across the unit.

## 5. Self-blind-solve (skeptical, passage only)

Solved both from the passage alone, arguing actively *for* each non-key.

- **Q1 → D, uniquely.** A is the only distractor with real pull, and it fails on
  an explicit sentence pair in P4 rather than on a judgement call. B is
  contradicted by "femtio öre var vad de flesta lade ut". C is contradicted by
  the whole of P3. No second defensible answer.
- **Q2 → B, uniquely.** The stress test here was C: could the etiquette book's
  advice be read as the main message? No — it is one sentence of one paragraph,
  and the option misstates what the advice was about, so it is wrong on content
  as well as on altitude. A was tested for partial defensibility (the passage
  *does* concede the almanac's role) and fails on the absolute plus the explicit
  "dröjde kvar i årtionden". D has zero textual purchase.

No item was two-way; no rewrite round was needed after the blind solve. Two
**language** defects were caught in the read-aloud pass and fixed:
`om riktningen är han säker` → `riktningen är han säker på` (Swedish takes
*säker **på***, not *om* — a G-SPRÅK-grade error), and an elliptical
`ville visa något` → `ville visa uppmärksamhet`. A plural
`Etikettböckernas` in Q2/C was made singular to match the single book in the
passage.

## 7. Names and law-16 verification — read the caveat

Collision surface was minimised by design: **two** named people, **no**
institution, **no** toponym, **no** named publication (the 1934 etiquette book
is titleless and authorless; the weekly press is generic). No real person is
given invented words.

Neither name appears in the addendum registry, and no given name repeats inside
the unit.

**Cross-unit given-name collision caught and fixed.** The essayist was first
drafted as *Gunvor* Malmryd. A check of the sibling batch15 drafts found
`gen-las-short-1.json` already using **Gunvor** Slättmar — two Gunvors in one
batch is precisely the batch14 "three Vendelas" defect that addendum §4 exists
to prevent, and the registry only covers *shipped* names, so nothing mechanical
would have caught it. The byline was renamed to **Barbro** Malmryd after
checking that *Barbro* appears zero times in the addendum registry, zero times
in the other batch15 drafts, and zero times across all 93 shipped units. If a
later repair round touches the sibling unit instead, this unit's byline is the
cheaper one to revert — it is a single occurrence.

**The search log is in `generator_meta.originality_note` and it is a log, not a
certificate.** Summary of its honest limits: this session's WebSearch budget was
already exhausted (200/200), DuckDuckGo via WebFetch returned HTTP 403, and
Firecrawl search returned HTTP 401. Verification therefore ran on Exa, which is
**semantic** search rather than exact-phrase search. Two candidate surnames were
searched and **rejected on real bearers** — *Vretmark* (Maria Vretmark,
Skaraborgs akademi) and *Alvengren* (Berra/Bert Alvengren, Svensk mediedatabas;
Linda Alvengren) — which is at least evidence the method surfaces collisions
when they exist. Searches for *Sixten Rimhall*, *Gunvor Malmryd* and (after the rename)
*Barbro Malmryd* surfaced no bearer of either surname. **I cannot certify that
an exact-phrase search would return the same result, and V-FINAL should re-run
both names.** Per law 16 this
is stated as an outcome with its method attached rather than as
"verified fictional".
