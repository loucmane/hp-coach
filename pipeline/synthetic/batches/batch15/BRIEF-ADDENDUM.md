## Tells cured in earlier batches — do not reintroduce

1. **Key-position shuffle (M-TELL / G-DISTRACTOR).** Raw batch14 seeds keyed
   ~16/18 answers at A. After drafting, shuffle option order so your keys land
   on different letters; across a multi-question unit no letter dominates.
2. **Quote typography (M-ECHO / G-REGISTER).** LÄS quotations use Swedish
   curly quotes ”…” exactly as the authentic corpus does — never straight ".
   ELF follows the English corpus convention in `elf/corpus-analysis.md`.
3. **Dash convention (M-ECHO).** The authentic corpus is ≈10 435 en dashes to
   1 em dash. Use spaced en dash ( – ) for parentheticals and ranges. NEVER
   em dash (—).
4. **Given-name diversity (law 13 / G-REGISTER).** Batch14 shipped three
   Vendelas before repair. Do not reuse ANY name from the registry below, and
   do not repeat a given name inside your own unit.
5. **Real-entity collision (law 16 / audit web-check).** Every invented
   person, institution, place and publication must be fictional AND
   non-colliding: a real same-domain entity with the same name is a REFUTE.
   Prefer clearly invented but Swedish-plausible surname blends (LÄS) /
   publication-neutral names (ELF).
6. **Option register (G-REGISTER).** LÄS options: max 21 words, no
   semicolons, no four-long-options-of-identical-shape rows. Authentic units
   include *short-breath* questions — in any multi-question unit, at least one
   question has a short stem and options of ≤8 words.
7. **ELF byline convention.** Long ELF passages carry the corpus-style
   source/byline line per `elf/blueprint-template.md`; shorts/cloze follow
   their blueprint exactly.
