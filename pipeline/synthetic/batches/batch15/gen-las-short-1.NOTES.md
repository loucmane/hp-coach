## 1. Topic choice, and why it is not one of the five suggested

The brief's primary requirement is *a fresh civic/municipal debate topic not on the excluded
list*; five candidates were offered as suggestions. Checked against the BRIEF-ADDENDUM's
93 shipped families (which bind: "do not pick the same topic, **an adjacent framing**, or the
same craft/industry lane"), all five graze something already shipped:

| suggested topic | graze |
|---|---|
| gatubelysning och trygghet | `dark-sky-observatory-lighting` (municipal lighting lane) **and** `grannsamverkan-brottsforebyggande-debatt` (trygghet lane) |
| kommunala odlingslotter | `stadsekologi-igelkott-popularvetenskap` — that shipped unit (las-b4-002) is *set inside koloniområden*, with lotter, staket and skötsel as its furniture |
| biblioteksmeröppet | `skolbibliotek-bemanning-debatt` — same debate axis (staffed vs unstaffed library hours) |
| offentliga dricksvattenfontäner | `offentliga-toaletter-debatt` — same framing (small municipal street facility quietly disappearing) |
| skolgårdars asfaltering | `playground-surface-fall-height` **and** `utomhuspedagogik-forskola-essa` |

So I stayed inside the assigned lane (civic/municipal debatt) but picked a topic with no
graze: **how a municipality allocates hall time in its gymnastiksalar to local associations**,
and whether standing bookings that renew themselves should be reviewed before a new hall is
built. Nothing in the 93 touches föreningsliv, lokalbokning, or municipal allocation
procedure. It is a real, recognisably Swedish municipal argument (kötid, säsongstider,
aktivitetsstöd, ideella styrelser), it carries concrete particulars, and the associations in
it are deliberately not only sports clubs' business — the fight is about administration, not
about sport, which keeps it clear of the three sports-adjacent shipped families.

## 2. Thesis shape and skeleton (law 12 — clone avoidance)

P5's shipped debatt/essä shorts (`las-b12-002`, `las-b13-002`, `las-b13-003`) run one mould:
reframing lede about how the debate is miscounted → researcher at an invented institute with
an inventory → the researcher's own caveat → an unexplained residual finding → a vignette
with a named person and an object → aphoristic two-sentence close. This unit breaks it at
every joint:

1. **Thesis shape = mechanism-is-the-point**, not "the metric measures the wrong thing"
   (capped at ~1/batch). The lede describes *how* a time is kept — the same form resubmitted
   each spring, and the form never asks whether the time was used.
2. **The opposing proposal is stated before the writer's own evidence**, fairly and with its
   strongest facts (real queue, twelve associations waiting, worn floor).
3. **No invented institute.** The review is done by kommunrevisionen through a named utredare.
4. **The objection wins.** It comes from the affected party (a föreningsordförande), not from
   a second researcher, and the writer changes her own proposal because of it ("det ändrar hur
   mitt förslag måste se ut") — the weekly-check version is dropped for an annual review.
5. **No vignette, no aphorism.** The text ends on an administrative detail (the form already
   has a box for planned weeks; nobody has had to fill it in) and simply stops.
6. Per law 15: **no announced objection** ("Invändningarna är inte tomma" style) and **no
   "Jag har X i N år" credential** — the byline carries the standing.
7. Friction kept in on purpose (law 9): the freed hours are late evenings and no use to a club
   of nine-year-olds. The writer concedes it and still refuses the hall; the residue does not
   point at either answer.

Surface variety: flat place-name title (no negation headline, no "not A but B"); numerals in
two registers (61 miljoner in digits, "Sextioen miljoner" spelled out, "drygt en femtedel",
"nio salar"); spaced en dash only (0 em dashes in the file); Swedish curly quotes ”…”;
byline + two-entry glossary, both terms occurring in the passage (`aktivitetsstöd`,
`säsongstid`).

## 3. Planted targets and trap architecture

**Planted target for Q1** (P3, hedged + directional + scoped): *drygt en femtedel* of booked
hours had no reported activity; the empty hours sat **framför allt** on passes renewed year
after year without reapplication, **while** times redistributed each term were used about as
much as booked; **afternoons barely affected**; two stated caveats (uneven reporting, only
full-season reporters counted) with the researcher's own conclusion that the figure is more
likely too low than too high.

| Q1 (`detalj_ospecificerad`) | trap | operation on the target |
|---|---|---|
| A | `reversed_causality` | swaps the two booking categories — puts the empty hours on the term-by-term applicants, which the passage explicitly denies |
| B | `overgeneralisation` | turns an aggregate share into "samtliga föreningar … varje vecka" |
| C | `scope_shift` (+ `detail_as_main`) | promotes one of the two caveats to "the main result", and inverts what Hasselgrund concludes from it |
| **D (key)** | — | paraphrase; "gått i arv" recasts "förnyats år efter år utan att sökas om" (no verbatim lift) |

**Planted target for Q2** (P2 + P5 + P6): the proponents' line ("inget annat än nya
kvadratmeter ger fler timmar"), the writer's break with it ("Där tar enigheten slut"), her
explicit stance ("Hallen vid Ekbacken behövs inte för att skapa de timmarna"), the annual
review that produces them, and her refusal to be moved by the late-evening problem.

| Q2 (`forfattarens_hallning`) | trap | operation |
|---|---|---|
| A | `plausible_worldknowledge` | "wait for more seasons of data" — a reasonable stance in general that she never takes; she wants it done by the next application round |
| **B (key)** | — | the stance she asserts, with its condition |
| C | `surface_lexical_echo` / attribution-swap | the proponents' own sentence offered as hers |
| D | `half_right_conjunction` | second clause is the passage's real residue (late hours), first clause is false — she rejects the hall outright, not "later" |

**Law 10, hidden-trap break.** In Q1 the key is the hedged, scoped option and the
overgeneralisation distractor is the absolute one — the usual polarity. In Q2 it is reversed:
the key is the flat, unhedged assertion and the *cautious* option (A, "wait for more figures")
is wrong. "Pick the qualified answer" therefore scores 1/2, not 2/2, on this unit.

## 4. Self-blind-solve (skeptical, passage only)

Solved both cold from the passage, arguing actively for every non-key.

- **Q1 → D.** A is contradicted by the clause about term-redistributed times (the passage puts
  those at near-full use). B needs a per-association, per-week claim the passage never makes;
  it reports one aggregate share. C is the strongest tempter because "rapporteringen håller
  ojämn kvalitet" is genuinely in the text — but it is one of two explicitly labelled
  förbehåll, and the very next sentence draws the opposite conclusion (the figure is more
  likely too low than too high, i.e. usable). Only D survives.
- **Q2 → B.** A asks for data she never asks for. C is a quoted opposing line the text
  immediately breaks with. D's second clause is true residue, but its first clause is refused
  in the closing paragraph — sixty-one million is, in her words, too much to pay to avoid the
  late-hours problem. Only B survives.

No two-way item found; nothing was rewritten for double-keying after this pass. One earlier
wobble *was* fixed: the passage first used "bortfallet" for the unused hours, which in a
paragraph about närvarorapporter could be read as survey non-response and would have made C
defensible. It now reads "de oanvända timmarna".

## 6. Names — verification log (law 16)

The session's WebSearch budget was exhausted, so the checks were run through Exa web search;
full queries, sources, dates and outcomes are recorded in `generator_meta.originality_note`
so they can be re-run. Summary:

- **Verner Hasselgrund** (utredare) — kept. Only hit for the string was a German kennel suffix
  in a dog pedigree; no person, none in municipal audit/administration.
- **Gunvor Slättmar** (föreningsordförande) — kept. No hits on the name at all.
- **Tora Steneryd** (byline) — kept. No bearer of the full name; the surname exists (a 1964
  secondary-school teacher of maths and physics) and Steneryd is a hamlet in Torhamn — both
  unrelated fields.
- **Verner Bråtnäs** — *rejected and replaced*: Bråtnäs is a real nature reserve in Dalsland
  and a locality in Sandviken kommun.
- **Berit Kvarnås** — *rejected and replaced*: a genealogy directory lists private individuals
  named Kvarnås/Kvarnäs, including a "berit".
- **Vässlinge** (municipality) — kept. No place of that name found; the search returned only
  Välinge, Utvälinge, Nävlinge, Vässingsö.
- **Not verified, disclosed:** *Ekbacken* and *Kyrkgatan* are generic Swedish place/street
  names occurring in many towns; they are used inside the fictional municipality and were not
  searched.

Gender assignment was randomised against the saturated careful-woman/overconfident-man
pattern: the careful researcher is a man, the challenger who wins her point is a woman, the
debattör is a woman. No given name is repeated inside the unit and none of the three names
(given or family) appears in the addendum registry.

## 7. Residual risks for the gates to weigh

- **Setting adjacency:** the unit shares "municipal facilities + associations" airspace with
  nothing shipped, but a reviewer who reads `folkbadhus-institutionshistoria` broadly as
  "municipal building for public use" could call it near. The framing here is allocation
  procedure, not institution history.
- Q1 option C is deliberately the hardest distractor; if a blind solver reads the two
  förbehåll as the report's conclusion rather than as caveats, it will be argued for. The
  sentence "Siffran är därför snarare för låg än för hög" is the discriminator and is placed
  immediately after them for that reason.
- The passage states its stance plainly (a debatt should), so Q2's difficulty rests entirely
  on the distractors, especially D's half-true second clause.
