## 1. Genre and subject — why this one

The binding bar for this slot is the heaviest in the LÄS lanes. Five consecutive
essä-shorts (b16-003 midsommarstången → b20-003 de handkolorerade vykorten) are
one skeleton: a complete documentary corpus from a defunct local body, counted in
the first person, one named custodian, a limits paragraph, a coda quoting a last
document. Batch21 broke the archive frame and landed on a domestic room instead
(b21-003 finrummet), which this brief also bars, together with furniture and
visiting custom.

What is left is an essay whose **evidence is not a counted collection**. This one
takes a **season and a physical condition**: *skaren* — the load-bearing crust
that forms on old snow in March when a thaw day is followed by a still, cloudless
night — and the narrow morning window in which it carries.

It is a good subject for this lane for four reasons.

1. **There is nothing to count.** No holding, no register, no custodian, no
   institution. The evidence is a bodily practice (you test the crust with your
   heel and listen) and a mechanism (melt, refreeze, and a deadline set by the
   sun). Every numeric expression in the passage was enumerated on the final
   bytes — *två saker, en decimeter, några timmar, vid fyra, vid tio* (twice),
   *tredje steget, tre veckor, fyra morgnar, ett par vintrar, ett par veckor,
   två upphöjda ryggar* — and not one of them counts a holding.
2. **It goes indoors nowhere.** The only buildings named are a stable the horse
   stayed in and a neighbour's barn used as a landmark from the outside.
3. **It carries its own difficulty.** The crust inverts two ordinary intuitions
   — that a horse is the strong option (a hoof concentrates the whole animal on
   a dinner-plate of surface and goes through where a man walks) and that frozen
   snow is slippery (at dawn it is *sträv som fint grus*; it turns glossy only
   later, when it has already stopped bearing).
4. **It ends resolved.** Ploughed roads and machines removed the need; the
   practice did not decay mysteriously, it became unnecessary, and the essay says
   so flatly before closing on an image.

**Thesis shape: mechanism-is-the-point, resolved.** The physics is the content,
and everything social — which animal could be used, how far a farm could reach,
why nothing could be scheduled — is derived from it. Checked against the six
sibling lane briefs: it is not a metric critique, not an institution in decline,
not a received explanation demoted for a timing fact (the b21 essä/cloze
convergence the brief names), not a written instrument that fails to govern, not
an institutional record with counts, not a service withdrawal.

**Opening move: procedural test.** The reader presses a heel into the snow and
listens for one of two sounds. No scene, no definition, no case-lede, no
result-lede, no framing claim about a district — and, per RULE 24, **no
received-explanation opening**.

**Coda: concrete and resolved.** Sledge-runner tracks stand proud of the snow
that has sunk around them, so at midday the morning's route is legible as two
raised ridges across the mire. Not an aphorism, not a date, not an
administrative fact, not a question, not deliberately unresolved — and it does
not use **`kvar`** (zero occurrences under `$.title`, `$.passage`,
`$.questions[*].prompt`, `$.questions[*].options[*].text`,
`$.questions[*].rationale`).

**Law 9 residue that points at nothing:** the spruce shade where the crust never
forms, the south slopes that fail first, the people who could read a slope at a
distance and the people who had to follow them, and *menföre* — the weeks after
the crust when the ground bears neither sledge nor wheel.

## 2. Trap architecture

### q1 — *Vad sägs i texten om hästarna?* (`enligt_texten_detalj`, short-breath)

| | option | trap | the span it dies on |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Hovarna sjönk igenom där folk gick. | **KEY** — paraphrase of the load-per-area explanation | *En hov samlar hela djurets tyngd på en yta stor som en tallrik* + *Skaren som bar en karl gick sönder under hoven* |
| B | Hovarna fick broddar inför de tyngsta lassen. | surface-lexical echo + affirming a rejected remedy | *mot det hjälper varken broddar eller lättare lass* |
| C | Hästarna ersattes av folk på skidor. | half-right conjunction — right replacement, wrong means | *kälken drogs till fots* + *Skidor dög till att ta sig fram med, men inte till att dra med* |
| D | Hovarna halkade på den blanka ytan. | plausible world-knowledge + wrong property | *Skaren är inte hal om morgonen* + *sträv som fint grus under sulan* |

B is the strongest distractor and is meant to be: studding draught horses in
winter is a real Swedish practice, and the word *broddar* appears in the text —
inside the clause that dismisses it. This is the shape RULE 23 asks for: the
distractor steals material from the passage and attaches it to the wrong side.

### q2 — *Vad framgår av texten om hur arbetet lades upp?* (`inference_slutsats`)

| | option | trap | the span it dies on |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Hushållen turades om och delade på vändorna. | plausible world-knowledge (collective winter work) | *Var gård körde för sig, och sällan väntade någon in grannen* |
| **B** | Det gick inte att bestämma dagarna i förväg. | **KEY** — inference, never stated | built from *Veckorna kom när de kom*, the two unequal years, and *bestämde sig kvällen innan* |
| C | Det mesta hanns med under en enda lång natt. | scope shift — collapses several mornings into one night | *Man ger sig i väg vid fyra, i mörker, och vid tio är det över* + *liggande till nästa natt* |
| D | Vintrar med sammanhängande kyla gjorde arbetet lättare. | reversed condition | *Vintrar då kylan håller i sig utan avbrott lämnar snön lös hela vägen ner* |

C is the trap for the fast reader: *i mörker* pulls toward "night", and it sits
in the very sentence that also gives the ten-o'clock end — the misreading is
punished by the clause that caused it.

## 3. RULE 15 — measured as a pair, both mechanisms

**Variables.** q1's variable is physical (why a horse could not be used on a
surface that carried a man). q2's variable is temporal-organisational (whether
the work could be scheduled). Neither is a mechanism inventory and neither is a
stance over the other's hypothesis space, so the las-b19-003 failure shape cannot
arise.

**(a) One-way leak, q1 → q2.** Option by option, what does each hand a
passage-blind reader about *scheduling*? A (hoofs sank where people walked) —
nothing. B (studs before the heaviest loads) — nothing beyond "loads existed".
C (people on skis replaced the horses) — nothing about when or how often.
D (hoofs slipped on the glossy surface) — nothing. **No proposition about q2's
variable is carried by even one option**, so the strong form the brief bars — a
proposition carried by two or more options, which survives non-resolution — is
absent.

**One-way leak, q2 → q1.** A (households took turns) — about who, not about what
pulled. B (days could not be fixed) — about the calendar. C (one long night) —
about duration. D (unbroken cold made it easier) — about weather. None
presupposes anything about hoofs, studs, skis or grip. No option contains a
concessive clause of the batch20 type (*även om …*) conceding a fact the other
item tests; the construction is absent from both sets.

**Stem leak.** Neither stem supplies a contested proposition. *hästarna* asserts
only that horses existed here; *hur arbetet lades upp* only that there was work.
Contrast batch21's essä, whose stem *det stängda rummet* handed the other item
its answer.

**(b) Symmetric vouching.** The two option sets are answered from **different
paragraphs**: q1 from paragraph 4 (hoof pressure, studs, hand sledge versus skis,
the crust's texture); q2 from paragraphs 2, 3 and 5 (the weather sequence, the
four-to-ten window, the unequal years, the evening decision, each farm on its
own). No wrong option in either set names a thing the other question turns on,
so members of one set cannot corroborate members of the other. The widening is
by construction, not by pruning.

**Joint stems-only estimate (honest).** Given only the two stems and the eight
options: q1 ≈ **0.30–0.35** — world knowledge makes A attractive, but B
(Swedish draught horses really were studded in winter) and D (crust reads as ice)
hold the field open. q2 ≈ **0.35–0.40** — "weather-dependent work cannot be
diarised" points at B, but D is the folk-plausible answer and A the expected
rural picture. **Joint, both right ≈ 0.12 (about 1 in 8)**; per item the pair
floor is about **1 in 3**, i.e. no better than 1-in-2 in the solver's favour,
which is inside the stated stance-composition tolerance. Reported as a joint
figure, not as two separate per-question floors.

## 6. Self-blind-solve

Passage read alone, keys covered, every non-keyed option argued for before the
key was consulted.

- **q1 → A, single defensible.** B is refuted in the same sentence that supplies
  the key's reason — the studs are named only to be dismissed — so a careful

  explicitly (*Skidor dög till att ta sig fram med, men inte till att dra med*).
  D dies on a flat denial plus the positive description that replaces it.

  "this could not be planned". It says the weeks came when they came, gives two

  evening's sky. A is contradicted in one clause, C by the stated start and end
  plus the counting in mornings, D by the passage's own condition for crust.
- **Keys spread:** A then B. No all-A column, no positional run.

## 10. Swedish read-aloud pass

Read aloud sentence by sentence, then re-read two sentences either side of every
cut. Fixed in the process:

- **Tense mixing inside one sentence** — *ett djur som trampar igenom skär upp
  benen* beside a past main clause became *trampade … skar*.
- **Fragment** — the two-step recipe was a verbless sentence after a full stop;
  joined to its head clause with a colon (*två saker som måste komma i rätt
  ordning: först … sedan …*).
- **Antecedent strain** — *ett par vintrar uteblev den* reached back over an
  intervening plural for its *den*; the noun was restored (*uteblev skaren*).
- **Repetition** — *Veckorna kom … Somliga år kom …* → *låg*; *Regn ger i
  stället …* beside *Lassen gick på kälke i stället* → *däremot*; *Snön … medan
  snön som …* → *medan det som kälkmedarna packat*; *tre veckor* twice → *ett par
  veckor* in ¶6; *Somliga* three times → *En del* in ¶6.
- **Relative `som` kept everywhere**, including where Swedish would allow an
  object relative to drop it (*det som kälkmedarna packat*).
