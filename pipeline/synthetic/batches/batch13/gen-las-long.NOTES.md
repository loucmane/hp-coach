# gen-las-long — authoring notes (Batch 13)

**Unit:** LÄS long, 4 questions. `family: stenmurar-odlingslandskap-popularvetenskap-long`
**Title:** *Två slags lämningar i samma sten*
**Topic (pool-assigned):** stenmurarna i odlingslandskapet — biologiskt och kulturellt arv,
röjningsrösen, murarnas ekologi. All landscapes, researchers, inventories and numbers invented.

## Genre and topic rationale

`sakprosa / populärvetenskap`, result-lede. The topic offers two independent evidentiary
strands — an ecological inventory and an archaeological re-dating — which lets the unit carry
four keys that assert genuinely different propositions instead of four readings of one finding.
It also gives a natural non-tidy ending: a legal protection that covers the stone but not the
land use that (maybe) produces the value, with the *why* left unsettled between two researchers.

Deliberate distance from the pool's forbidden neighbours: nothing here turns on pollinators or
insects as a cause, nothing on urban greenery, nothing on allemansrätt or on beavers/rivers.
The lede explicitly denies the intuitive "old and long = rich" thesis, so no famous real thesis
is being restated (law 1). Every named entity is fictional: Maud Ekelin, Gunhild Aspegren,
Sixten Frejmark, Torkel Almersson (byline), Sydsvenska landskapsinstitutet, Vråkeryds härad,
Ödesmåla, Sjöhaga, Kvistberga, Tälleryd, Rävsmåla.

Frame (law 6): title in `title` only; byline last inside `passage`; three-entry glossary at the
tail defining **röjningsröse**, **borrmärke**, **biotopskydd** — all three occur in the text.

Anti-tidiness (law 9): the temperature measurement covers 11 walls that are *not* a subset of
the 200 surveyed; the land-use classification comes from a 1937 register with wartime gaps; only
walls visible on 1961 aerial photos are in the material; the 200 are the ones whose owners
consented. Paragraph 7 is pure residue (a parish-boundary dispute, numbered gate posts, a broken
quern, 300 m of wall sold as road fill at three kronor a cubic metre, an unreadable inscription)
and points at no answer.

## Planted targets and trap architecture

**Target 1 (p1, hedged + directional + scoped):** walls with *different* land use on their two
sides "tycks bära en artrikare kant" — but only where the wall lies open to the south.

**Q1 — `enligt_texten_detalj`, key B.** Key paraphrases the target keeping hedge, direction and
scope.
- A `reversed_comparison`: the comparison is flipped (same land use on both sides = richest).
  Carries the same "tycks" as the key, so hedge-picking does not help.
- C `overgeneralisation`: hedge → "ger", scope stripped ("varje mur", "oberoende av hur den
  ligger i terrängen").
- D `plausible_worldknowledge`: length and age decisive — the intuitive prior the lede denies.

**Target 2 (p3):** the re-dating criterion. 340 → drygt 200 because drill-marked, split blocks
were found in two of five cairns, and blasting reached the district only from the 1840s
(340 × 3/5 ≈ 204 — the numbers reconcile).

**Q2 — `detalj_ospecificerad`, key D (confident, specific).** Stem names the setup (the count was
revised down) without entailing the criterion.
- A `plausible_worldknowledge`: carbon/pollen dating — the method a reader expects from
  archaeology; absent from the text.
- B `negated_target_restated`: form and location, which the passage explicitly excludes
  ("Avgörandet låg varken i formen eller i läget").
- C `refuted_agemarker_overhedged`: moss cover, which the text disqualifies — and the only
  hedged option in the set, so hedge-picking fails here.

**Target 3 (p6):** Frejmark's rival explanation — the walls separating pasture from arable are
the old boundary walls nobody took stone from, so undisturbedness, not the land-use contrast,
may explain the counts. Ekelin's counter-evidence (11 boundary walls with uniform land use
since the seventies) and his reply (thirty years is short for a lichen) leave it unresolved:
"De två förklaringarna pekar ut samma murar, och det som mätts skiljer dem inte åt."

**Q3 — `detalj_ospecificerad`, key A (confident).**
- B `overgeneralisation`: a method complaint inflated into "no relation can be tested at all".
- C `attribution_swap`: the consent-based sample skew is real but is *Ekelin's own* stated
  limitation.
- D `plausible_worldknowledge`: lichens as air-quality indicators — genuinely known, wholly
  absent from the passage.

**Q4 — `huvudbudskap_syfte`, key C.** Spans p1 (dual heritage), p6 (unresolved dispute) and p8
(the protection covers the stone, nothing obliges anyone to keep grazing; half the walls now
have the same land use on both sides; Rävsmåla would have been saved, the richest walls
unaffected). Law 11: every distractor carries a locatable flaw.
- A `detail_as_main` + unsupported evaluative leap ("mindre värdefullt kulturarv") — and it is
  the only hedged option in the set while the key is flat.
- B `overgeneralisation_av_oenigheten`: the text says the measurements do *not* separate the two
  explanations, so neither "utesluter varandra" nor "materialet visar" survives.
- D `detail_as_main_forvriden_vikt`: "framför allt" turns one of two simultaneous purposes
  ("ett hägn och en avstjälpningsplats", plus the fencing duty that came with the skifte) into
  the main one.

## Hedge balance (law 10, second half)

Key hedged only in Q1; confident in Q2, Q3, Q4. In Q1 the reversed-comparison distractor carries
the identical "tycks"; in Q2 the sole hedged option (C) is wrong; in Q4 the sole hedged option
(A) is wrong. "Pick the qualified answer" scores at or below chance across the sheet.

## Cross-question corroboration guard

Keys assert four different propositions: the scoped land-use-contrast finding; the blasted-stone
dating criterion; Frejmark's undisturbedness confounder; the whole-text verdict about protection
vs. the contested source of value. Q3's options were rewritten to avoid naming *markslag* or sun
exposure at all, so they cannot corroborate Q1's key; Q4's distractors concern cairn age, the
strength of the disagreement, and why the walls were built — none of which touch Q1's finding or
Q2's criterion. The two `plausible_worldknowledge` distractors act on different domains (wall
size; air quality), so no trap pattern generalises.

## Self-blind-solve result

Solved all four from the passage alone, arguing actively for each non-keyed option before
rejecting it. Exactly one defensible answer per question: **Q1 = B, Q2 = D, Q3 = A, Q4 = C.**
Key spread B, D, A, C — no positional tell. Nothing on the sheet is answerable from real-world
knowledge of Swedish stone walls, clearance cairns or biotope protection; every discriminating
fact is passage-internal and invented.
