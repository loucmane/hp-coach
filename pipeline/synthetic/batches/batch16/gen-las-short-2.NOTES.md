## 1. Topic and lane

**Topic:** why neighbouring villages keep visibly different midsummer-pole
forms, and what actually carries the form from one summer to the next.

From the assigned candidate list I took **midsommarstångens lokala
variationer**. Rejected the other four in the lane brief:

- *folkdräktens återkomst* — grazes `tatueringsnormalisering-essa-short`
  (visible body/dress normalised in the present, meaning drained).
- *kaffekalasets dramaturgi* — grazes batch15's `namnsdagsseden-essa-short`
  on exactly the framing the brief forbids (hospitality, what you owe a
  guest, the small gift).
- *veckotidningarnas följetonger* — namnsdagsseden's evidence base was
  *veckopressens frågespalter*; reusing the veckopress reads as a graze.
- *sommarkoloniernas vardag* — that is the philanthropic-institution-in-
  decline form the brief flags as saturated (`folkbadhus-`,
  `folkbildningshistoria-`, `folkrörelsebyggnad`).

**Graze check against the 100-family exclusion list:** no festival, folk-
custom-object or material-folk-culture family is on it. The two nearest are
`namnsdagsseden-essa-short` (calendar custom) and `flaggsed-debatt-short`
(national symbol, debatt genre). Separation from namnsdagsseden is
deliberate and total on the axis that matters: that unit's mechanism is a
**price ceiling that kept the custom socially wide**; this unit's mechanism
is **a physical object that carries a form without anyone remembering or
deciding**. No gift, no uppvaktning, no almanacka, no social reach, no
etiquette book.

**Law 1 / famous-thesis guard:** the obvious real thesis here is
Hobsbawm-style *invented tradition* (and the Skansen/nationalromantik
story). Both were consciously avoided — the essay never asks whether the
custom is old or authentic. A knowledgeable solver gains nothing: the
mechanism (a stored wooden frame is an instruction) is invented and
passage-specific.

## 6. Self-blind-solve (skeptical, from the passage alone)

Solved both cold, arguing actively **for** each non-keyed option.

- **Q1 = B, uniquely.** A is the stated direction inverted. C belongs to the
  stored-frame group. D has no textual basis. I could not build a case for
  any of the three.
- **Q2 = D, uniquely.** The hardest defence to break was **C**: the passage
  does hedge (*tycks*) and does confess coverage gaps. It fails anyway,

  material *cannot* answer why misstates the text. B is the next-strongest
  and dies on the Rossmåla lövning. A dies on the keeper's quote.
- **Cross-question corroboration: none.** Q1 tests the drift *direction*;
  Q2 tests the whole-text *mechanism*. Q2's key does not disclose whether
  unstored forms converge or diverge, and Q1's key does not eliminate any
  of Q2's options.

## 7. Clone avoidance (law 12)

The three shipped P5 essä-shorts (`las-b15-003` namnsdagsseden,
`las-b9-003` offentliga-ur, `las-b13-002` tatueringsnormalisering) share
one mould: *critique-of-a-naive-measure lede → named academic ethnologist
with an archive corpus and percentages → hedged, unevenly distributed
finding → "den vanliga förklaringen räcker inte" inversion → skeptic or
self-caveat paragraph → personal or aphoristic coda*.

This unit breaks it at every joint:

- **lede:** scene, three concrete objects, no metric to debunk;
- **evidence:** an amateur folkskollärare with a bicycle, a tape measure
  and a waxcloth notebook — no institute, no percentages, no interviews;
- **thesis shape:** *mechanism-is-the-point* (the object transmits the
  form), not *the-metric-measures-the-wrong-thing*, and **no
  inversion-of-a-common-view move**;
- **skeptic slot:** **empty** — no external challenger; the friction comes
  from residue that refines the scope;
- **coda:** a flat administrative exchange (a request, a refusal). It does
  not return to the opening image, it is not an aphorism, and it is not a
  relative's anecdote.

Blocklist (law 14) checked: none of the burned phrase families or close
variants appear — in particular no *"Materialet/Metoden har sina hål"*
(the coverage limit is stated as concrete facts instead) and no *"not A,
but B"* chiasmus in the coda. M-ECHO against all 100 shipped units: pass.
