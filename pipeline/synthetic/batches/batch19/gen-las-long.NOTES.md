## 1. Genre / topic rationale

`sakprosa / facktext_larobok`, long (975 words, 49 sentences, mean 19.9 w/s,
10 paragraphs). Topic (b) was chosen over (a) pottaskebränning and (c)
vadmalsstampar for two reasons:

* **(a) risked topical adjacency** to the excluded burn-craft cluster
  (kalkbränning shipped as batch18's LÄS long; kolning/tjärdalar shipped as
  las-b14-001; tegelbruk, tändstickor). Pottaskebränning is a third
  "wood into the fire, ash into a barrel" craft and would have grazed the
  same mould.
* **(c) risked motif adjacency** to the saturated shared-work-rotation motif:
  batch18 shipped *both* `kyrkbatslag-roddordning-essa-short` (a rowing
  turn-order) and a kalkbränning passage whose organisation section is an
  `ugnslag` that "turades om vid elden och delade … efter insats". A
  vadmalsstamp passage about *socknarnas turordning* is the same motif twice
  in consecutive batches.

Topic (b) also happens to be the best possible carrier for the assigned
**mechanism-is-the-point** thesis shape: the whole shape of pre-blast-furnace
iron — small output per run, spongy slag-filled blooms, labour concentrated in
the smithy rather than at the furnace, production spread across parishes rather
than concentrated at works — follows from one physical fact, that the bloomery
reduced the ore **in the solid state** and never melted the metal. The passage
is built so that the thesis is that mechanism, not a moral about it.

## 3. Trap architecture (planted targets → distractor operations)

Passage and questions were designed together; every planted target is hedged,
directional and scoped, and every distractor is a named operation on it.

### Q1 — `enligt_texten_detalj` — **key C**
*Stem:* ”Vad säger texten om hur malmen togs upp och gjordes i ordning?”
(neutral referential — names the referent, predicates nothing about it: rule 12)

*Target:* ¶1's explicit ordering — late autumn dig (when the water has dropped)
→ heaped on firm ground → **left out over the winter** so frost breaks the lumps
→ *only then* roasted.

* **A — scope_shift / order-inversion.** Moves extraction to late winter "medan
  tjälen bar" and puts roasting *before* heaping. Deliberately the strongest
  rival: heavy winter haulage over frozen ground is real-world true of Swedish
  rural craft (and batch18's kalk passage says exactly that about its own
  material), so a knowledgeable solver is *pulled* here. The passage refutes it
  sentence-by-sentence.
* **B — overgeneralisation.** "Varje mosse i socknen bar ett skikt". ¶2 states
  the opposite in as many words: *Långt ifrån varje mosse bär ett sådant
  skikt, och de som gör det bär det ojämnt.*
* **D — plausible_worldknowledge, deliberately OVER-HEDGED.** "Ibland kunde
  malmen tas upp utan att skiktet först hade letats upp med spett, om marken var
  tillräckligt blöt." ¶1 says the layer could not be found without the rod
  because it does not show at the surface. This is the rule-13 / law-10
  counter-weight: the *qualified* option here is false, so "pick the hedged one"
  misfires on Q1.

### Q2 — `detalj_ospecificerad` (content-anchored on the named trials) — **key A**
*Stem:* ”Vad framkom i de blåsningar som Tolvsäter lät göra?”

*Target:* ¶7's first of two results — the hardest-driven runs gave not larger
but **more brittle** blooms, because carbon entered the iron.

* **B — reversed_causality.** Same two quantities, arrow flipped (weaker
  driving → brittler iron). The corpus's #1 trap, and the mirror is genuinely
  tempting because both directions are internally coherent.
* **C — overgeneralisation.** "Samtliga blåsningar misslyckades…" The passage
  reports a *worse but usable* outcome, not failure.
* **D — scope_shift (method as result).** Re-erecting furnaces is the trial
  *setup*; ¶3's furnace-drying sentence is planted specifically to give this
  distractor a surface lexical foothold. "framför allt" is the identifiable
  flaw — the passage names two other things as the results.

### Q3 — `huvudbudskap_syfte` — **key D**
*Stem:* ”Vilket påstående överensstämmer bäst med texten?” (corpus-attested)

*Target:* the whole-text span — ¶3 (temperature melts the slag, not the metal),
¶4 (solid-state reduction, a lump that never ran), ¶5 (a few kilos per run;
the weight of the work sits in the räckning afterwards).

Law-11 discipline: **no distractor here is verbatim-true.** Each carries a
locatable flaw:

* **A — detail_as_main + quantifier upgrade.** Cheap plant → self-supply is a
  one-paragraph sub-point, and "varje bygd" is refuted by ¶2 (far from every
  mire carries ore, so far from every district could).
* **B — overgeneralisation.** "i alla stycken underlägsen" — the passage never
  ranks the two processes; it gives the blast furnace *different* requirements
  and an *extra* step (tackjärnet must be färskat before it can be forged).
* **C — scope_shift.** The uppteckningar are used for exactly one datum
  (ore-layer regrowth) and ¶8 says outright that they say almost nothing about
  the smelt itself; the knowledge base is excavation plus trials.

### Q4 — `inference_slutsats` — **key B**
*Stem:* ”Vad kan man, utifrån texten, dra för slutsats om järnhalten i slaggen?”
(neutral referential — names the quantity without predicating anything about it)

*Target:* requires combining **two** passage facts in the stated direction —
(i) a lower-iron slag becomes viscous at the temperatures such a furnace can
hold, and (ii) a slag that does not run stays around the grains and makes the
bloom impossible to clean. Therefore the iron in the waste is a *condition*, not
a loss. This is the only key that is hedged ("snarast").

* **A — plausible_worldknowledge.** This is Krokvall's own reading, which the
  passage argues against. Note the trap's construction: its *factual* half is
  true (a large share did stay in the waste) — the flaw is the attribution to
  carelessness.
* **C — overgeneralisation + wrong method.** "densamma på samtliga undersökta
  platser"; the passage dates the sites by charcoal from the tapping pits and
  reports large between-site variation.
* **D — half_right_conjunction.** Second clause explicitly refuted: ¶8 says
  neither ore grade nor furnace size accounts for the spread in yield.

**Key spread:** C, A, D, B (all four letters; no positional column).
**M-TELL:** the key is the single longest option in **0 of 4** questions.
**M-FORM:** on every question at least one distractor is absolutizer-free, so
"strip the absolutes and pick what's left" never resolves the sheet.
**Rule 13 / law 10 hedge balance:** keys on Q1–Q3 are confident, absolute-free
specific claims; only Q4's key is hedged. Q1 D is an over-hedged **false**
distractor, so "correct" and "qualified" do not line up across the unit.

## 6. Self-blind-solve

Solved twice from the passage alone, arguing actively for every non-keyed
option, with the rationales covered.

* **Q1 → C.** A is the real contender and I argued it hard: winter haulage on
  frozen ground is a documented practice in this craft family, so "vårvintern,
  medan tjälen bar" *sounds* like the right kind of fact. It fails on ¶1's
  explicit chain (late autumn dig → out over the winter → *först därefter*
  roasted): A both moves the season and inverts the order. B dies on ¶2's
  "Långt ifrån varje mosse". D dies on ¶1's "Utan spettet gick skiktet inte att
  finna". One defensible answer.
* **Q2 → A.** B is A's mirror and the passage states the direction outright
  ("De blåsningar som drevs hårdast … gav inte större luppar utan sprödare");
  only one of the two can stand. C over-reads "sprödare" as "misslyckades". D is
  about the setup, and "framför allt" makes it false against "Två resultat är
  tydliga". One defensible answer.
* **Q3 → D.** A is the dangerous one because its first clause is supported;
  it fails as a *whole-text* summary (one paragraph's sub-point) and its
  quantifier is refuted by ¶2. B mis-states a comparison the passage never
  makes. C is contradicted by ¶8's explicit statement about what the
  uppteckningar do and do not carry. D is the only option that spans ¶3, ¶4 and
  ¶5. One defensible answer.
* **Q4 → B.** A is the option a general reader reaches for, and it is the
  position the passage exists to argue against; its factual half is true, which
  is what makes it a trap rather than a throwaway. C is refuted twice (dating
  method; between-site variation). D's second clause is refuted verbatim in ¶8.
  B requires ¶7's two-step and is the only option consistent with it.

**Adversarial blind floor** (options only, no passage): Q1 no better than
1-in-2 (A vs C both read as plausible craft facts); Q2 1-in-2 at best (A/B are
a direction pair, and C's absolutizer is the only free elimination); Q3 A is at
least as attractive as D to a non-reader; Q4 A is the *more* attractive option
to a non-reader, so the blind floor is below chance-plus. No sheet-wide
elimination heuristic (longest option, hedged option, strip-the-absolutes)
resolves more than one question.

## 9. Morphology guard (the "bränndes" post-mortem)

Every past/passive form in the passage was read aloud and checked against the
rule that **-nn- and -mm- stems simplify before a dental suffix**
(känna→kändes, bränna→brändes, glömma→glömdes), while -ll- and -pp- do not
(ställa→ställde, släppa→släppte).

*The verb that killed a batch18 unit is not in this passage at all* — the ore is
**rostad / rostades**, never "bränd", and charcoal is a purchased input
(träkol), never a process the text describes. That was a deliberate choice: it
removes the failure mode instead of getting it right.

sjunkit, sprängt, sköljt, rostades, tändes, drevs (×2: *driva*→drevs and

drog, bakades, runnit, bröts, glödde, pressas, lagades, revs, restes, försvann,
smälte, krävde, färskas, flyttade, invänt, lät, låg, stannade, lämnade, läste,

framställs, framställdes.
