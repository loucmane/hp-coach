## 1. Topic and lane

**Topic:** the *handledning* — the twenty-page instruction booklet that shipped with
every series of Swedish classroom wall charts (skolplanscher), c. 1890–1958. The
essayist reads eighty-three of them out of a private collector's shelf and measures
one thing: what proportion of each booklet is questions the teacher is told to ask
versus finished statements the teacher is told to deliver. The proportion falls from
about two thirds to barely one third; the history series are the stated exception,
statement-heavy from the earliest volumes. The pictures move with the booklets — late
charts carry fewer incidental details in the margins — and because the same publishers
made both, the essayist reads the shift as deliberate rather than accidental.

**Why this topic.** The brief's four candidates were checked against the 107 excluded
families and against the shipped bank before writing:

- **landsbygdsauktionernas ekonomi och ritual** — *rejected.* `las-b14-002`
  (`sockenmagasin-recension-long`) already occupies rural pre-monetary credit: it calls
  the parish granary "en bank vars valuta var säd", runs a ledger-of-debts motif, and
  opens on cash scarcity in the countryside. An auction-as-credit-institution essay
  would have grazed it hard. A non-credit auction angle (goods circulating repeatedly)
  grazes the excluded ELF `repair-economy-secondhand-resale`.
- **syjuntornas tysta ekonomi** — *rejected.* Sewing-circle economics is hosting-rota
  and gift mechanics, which the brief explicitly fences off (namnsdagsseden took it).
- **julgransplundringens lekar och dramaturgi** — *rejected.* Double graze: a seasonal
  custom (midsommarstången's lane) with a gift/sweet-bag payload (namnsdagen's lane).
- **skolplanschernas bildvärld** — *taken.* The school-adjacent families in the bank are
  `utomhuspedagogik-forskola-essa-short` (outdoor-time metric critique),
  `slojd-i-skolan-essa-short` (craft and cognition), `folkbildningshistoria-facktext-long`
  (study-circle minute books), plus skolbibliotek / läxor / skolmat / skolskjuts debatt
  units. None of them touches teaching **pictures** or printed teaching material. The
  angle was narrowed from "bildvärld" to the *booklets* so the unit does not become a
  materiality-of-the-object story, which would have cloned batch16 (see §5).

## 2. Question design

| q | family | trap plan | key |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `detalj_ospecificerad` (short-breath) | A scope_shift + plausible_worldknowledge · B reversed_causality · D plausible_worldknowledge (hedged) | **C** |
| 2 | `huvudbudskap_syfte` | B detail_as_main + half_right_conjunction · C reversed_causality + half_right_conjunction · D overgeneralisation of a granted concession + detail_as_main | **A** |

Distribution is corpus-right for a short passage: one detail-retrieval question whose
answer sits in a single sentence, one higher-order question. Not two higher-order
questions (the blueprint forbids an all-higher-order short unit).

### Planted targets

- **Q1 target** — the exception sentence closing ¶3: *"Där dominerade beskeden redan i de
  tidigaste årgångarna, och andelen frågor förblir liten hela vägen."* Directional
  (statements dominate from the start), scoped (history series only), and deliberately
  **not** absolute — "liten", not "ingen". The key paraphrases it; the traps operate on it.
- **Q2 target** — the whole-text span: ¶1 (what a handledning was for), ¶3 (the measured
  drift, two thirds → barely one third), ¶4 (the picture side moves with it), ¶5 (the 1958
  booklet: eleven statements, a word list, one question, last, after the word list).

### Why each distractor tempts

**Q1**
- **A** "färre bilder än naturserierna" — the passage compares the two series on exactly
  one axis (question share). Image counts are never mentioned. A reader who remembers
  *that a comparison was made* but not *what was compared* lands here.
- **B** "allt fler frågor med tiden" — the inversion. The only direction the passage
  states is *falling* question share in the nature booklets; of the history series it
  says the share stays low throughout, i.e. neither rising nor falling. This is the
  reversed_causality move the corpus uses more than any other.
- **D** "skrevs troligen av lärarna själva" — real-world-plausible (a handledning reads
  like teacher's-lore) and hedged enough to feel safe. Contradicted twice: the booklets
  come from the two dominant publishers, and ¶4 states outright that the same publisher
  made both picture and booklet.

**Q2**
- **B** — genuinely true second half (the booklets were rarely kept, ¶1) welded to an
  invented first half (print runs are never mentioned), and the whole thing is a
  sub-point about the *collection* rather than the essay's message. Per law 11 the
  distractor is not verbatim-true: the print-run clause is the identifiable flaw.
- **C** — the sharpest one. "Planscherna förenklades" is *true* (¶4). "Frågorna blev fler"
  inverts the load-bearing clause. A solver who retains "something decreased, something
  simplified" without retaining *which* picks this.
- **D** — Bråtemo's own objection promoted to the text's message, and absolutised on the
  way: the essayist concedes it is hard to get around that a booklet is only a wish, then
  goes past it via the picture evidence. "Kan knappast säga något" is a general
  prohibition the essay does not sign.

## 3. Self-blind-solve (done skeptically, arguing for every non-key)

Solved both cold from the passage alone, actively building the case for each of the six
distractors before rejecting it.

- **Q1 = C, uniquely.** A has zero textual footing (no image counts anywhere). B contradicts
  the exception sentence twice over — the falling trend belongs to the nature booklets, and
  the history series are described as flat. D is refuted by "de två förlag som dominerade
  marknaden" and by "samma förlag gjorde båda". Only C survives, and it survives because
  ¶3's last sentence says exactly that in different words.
- **Q2 = A, uniquely.** B fails on the print-run clause and on scope. C fails on direction
  in the one clause that carries the point. D fails because the essay explicitly overcomes
  the objection it grants. A is the only option that touches ¶1, ¶3, ¶4 and ¶5 at once.

**Two-way check.** The item I was most worried about was Q2 D — a concession the text
really does grant. It was written with "knappast säga något" precisely so the flaw is
*overstatement of a granted limit*, not the limit itself; the essay's own next move
("Just därför säger de något, nämligen att förändringen var avsiktlig") is the refutation
and it is on the page. No rewrite was needed after the re-solve.

**Cross-question leakage test** (option lists only, passage withheld): Q1 is about one
series type, Q2 about the whole-text movement. Neither key is derivable from the other's
option set. The word *frågor* deliberately appears in Q1 B (distractor), Q2 A (key),
Q2 C (distractor) and Q2 D (distractor), so there is **no lexical bridge that connects
only the two keys** — that exclusive-bridge pattern (the word "tak" in both keys) is what
held `las-b15-003` in integrated review, and it was designed out here from the start.

## 5. Clone avoidance (law 12)

Diffed against the three most recent shipped essä-short units and the older essä moulds:

| unit | lede | evidence figure | thesis shape | skeptic slot | coda |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `las-b15-003` namnsdagsseden | definitional, on a custom | academic ethnologist, magazine columns | **inverts a common view** | none (self-concession) | personal grandmother anecdote |
| `las-b16-003` midsommarstänger | scene, three villages | amateur schoolteacher with a tape measure | **materiality determines form** (the stored frame *is* the instruction) | **none** | flat administrative exchange |
| `las-b13-002` / `las-b9-003` | critique of a naive measure | — | metric-measures-the-wrong-thing | — | — |
| **this unit** | **definitional, on an object nobody kept** | **a private collector's shelf** | **straight reconstruction of a measured drift, with a stated exception and an honest non-independence caveat** | **the source's own owner objects** | **post-by-post inventory of one booklet** |

Deliberate divergences: no metric critique and no inversion-of-a-common-view move; the
documentarian is not the finder of the pattern but its doubter; the thesis is neither
"the number measures the wrong thing" nor "the object decides" — an early draft that made
the *varnished linen* the cause of what a generation saw was scrapped precisely because it
was batch16's mechanism wearing different clothes; the coda neither returns to the opening
image, nor lands an aphorism, nor uses the "inte A utan B" chiasmus — it counts the
contents of one booklet and stops.

**Gender randomisation:** the cautious, objecting voice is a man (Vilgot Bråtemo) and the
one advancing the reading is a woman (Alfhild Bräddlund, the byline). This inverts the
careful-woman / confident-man pattern the 2026-07-30 whole-bank scan found 18 times in 18.

## 6. Names, entities and law-16 verification

**Two person names in the whole unit. No place names at all. No named institution, no
named publisher, no named series, no named school.** That was a deliberate reduction of
collision surface after three toponym candidates failed (see below) and after
`las-b15-003` demonstrated that a toponym-free essä ships clean.

- **Vilgot Bråtemo** — the collector. Brave exact-phrase `"Bråtemo"` → NO RESULTS (three
  hits, all diacritic-free `bratemo`/`BRatemo`: a DeviantArt account, a domain reseller,
  an X account for Ben Ratemo). Exa → only the Greek-Cypriot `Bratemo`/`Bratemos`
  (BRATEMO LTD London; Stello Alexander Bratemos, San Francisco 1886; publican Frank
  Bratemos, Plymouth 1919) — no Swedish bearer of the diacritic form. Swedish Wikipedia
  full-text → "Inga resultat matchade frågan." Marginalia → NO RESULTS (weak: English-only
  index).
- **Alfhild Bräddlund** — the byline essayist. Brave exact-phrase `"Bräddlund"` →
  "Too few matches were found" (returned Brattlund, Brännlund, Brålanda, Björnlunda,
  Bramstedtlund). Exa → Brådd, Brattlund (38 bearers per hitta.se), Brännlund; no
  Bräddlund. Swedish Wikipedia full-text → "Inga resultat matchade frågan."
  *Flagged for V-FINAL:* Bräddlund sits two letters from the real surname Brattlund. Not a
  collision, but less distinctive than law 16 would prefer.
- **Given names** Vilgot and Alfhild are absent from the batch17 list of 231 used given
  names, are not near-duplicates of anything listed (nearest: Valfrid; Alma/Algot/Alrik),

**Tooling honesty (batch17 rule 4 / law 16).** This session's WebSearch budget was already
exhausted (200/200) at the first exact-phrase attempt — both `"Bräddlund"` and `"Bråtemo"`
came back with the budget message, not with results. The batch17 fallback recipe names
Mojeek + Brave; **Mojeek returned HTTP 403 on every query** and `firecrawl_search` returned
HTTP 401, so the two indices actually exercised were **Brave** (exact-phrase, via web-fetch)
and **Exa** (semantic — weaker than a quoted search), supplemented by Swedish Wikipedia
full-text and Marginalia. The two **full-name** phrases `"Vilgot Bråtemo"` and
`"Alfhild Bräddlund"` were **not** separately phrase-searched (Brave 429'd on that attempt);
since neither surname has any bearer in the indices that did run, no full name can either —
but that is an inference, not a search result. **Every kept name is flagged for V-FINAL
re-verification with exact-phrase WebSearch.**

**No real person is given invented words.** The two quoted fragments in ¶3 are lines from a
fictional booklet; Bråtemo's objection in ¶4 is reported indirectly. No famous thesis is
anchored: that teaching material drifts toward transmission is not named as anyone's theory,
and the measurable form of it here — question share across eighty-three booklets from two
publishers, with the history series as a stated exception — is invented for this passage,
as are all its numbers.

## 9. Residue for the reviewers

- Q2 D grants a real limitation of the source. It is defeated by an argument the essay
  makes explicitly, but it is the option most likely to draw a MULTIPLE_DEFENSIBLE flag,
  and it is the one to look at first.
- The essay does not claim the two evidence strands are independent — it says outright
  that the same publisher made both, and uses that to argue intent rather than accident.
  If a reviewer reads that as a logical slip rather than as an honest caveat, it is the
  second thing to look at.
- `Bräddlund` is two letters from the real `Brattlund`. V-FINAL may prefer a rename;
  nothing in the answer design depends on the surname.
