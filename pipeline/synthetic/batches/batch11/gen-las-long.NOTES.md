# gen-las-long — authoring notes (Batch 11)

**Unit:** LÄS long, 4 questions. `family: apotekshistoria-facktext-long`.
**Topic (exclusive to this generator):** apotekens historia — farmakopé,
apotekarprivilegier och apoteksväsendets förvandling. All apotek, towns,
persons, archives, years-of-record and numbers are invented.

## Genre and topic rationale

`sakprosa / facktext_larobok`, opening move = **result-lede**: the passage opens
on a researcher who went looking for one thing (recipes / medicines) and found
the ledgers answering another question (a counter full of soap, spices, dye and
leeches). This is the corpus's dominant LÄS shape — a dated, named, invented
study with method, finding, caveat, counter-argument and residue — and it lets
every question hang on a *passage-internal* fact rather than on anything a
knowledgeable solver could bring from outside. Pharmacy history is a real
domain, but no real farmakopé edition, institution, statute or scholar is named:
the governing body is a fictional *Hälsokollegiet*, the privilege letter is
Vretstad 1810, the recording reform is *1874 års taxa*, the archive is fourteen
invented landsortsapotek in a fictional *Nordanviks län*. Nothing in the unit is
answerable from real history; the one piece of shared world knowledge (that the
privilege system was eventually abolished) is placed in the closing paragraph
as **the story the text sets aside**, and no question turns on it.

Register: nominalisations (*lagerhållning*, *upptagningsområde*, *bokföringssättet*),
`-s`-passives (*bokfördes*, *reviderades*, *uppfylldes*), specialist lexicon
glossed in the tail (`farmakopé`, `privilegiebrev`, `visitation` — all three
occur in the passage). Sentence lengths deliberately uneven (3–48 words; e.g.
the four-word verdict *Journalerna svarade på en annan fråga.* beside a 48-word
finding sentence). Frame: title line in `title`, byline *— Ragnhild Öberg,
idéhistoriker* last inside `passage`, glossary at the very tail.

**Anti-tidiness:** the passage carries residue that serves no thesis — forty
leeches dead in transport, a pharmacist logging the harbour freeze-up for nine
winters, four skålpund of candles to the church, the 1867 line reading *"ingen
kom"* — plus genuine non-uniformity (four of fourteen apotek show nothing; one,
Timglaset i Skedvik, moves the other way) and a counter-argument the author
concedes bites (Abrahamsson's steamer-quay confound and the bookkeeping-change
artefact after 1874).

## Planted targets and trap architecture

The core planted claim is **hedged + directional + scoped**: *"på de apotek vars
privilegieområde hade krympt … **tycks** handeln med annat ha vuxit under
decennierna därefter — men **bara på de mindre orterna**, där det varken fanns
garnison eller lasarett."*

| q | family | key | trap plan |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `enligt_texten_detalj` | **C** | A `reversed_causality` (trade share → district cut, arrow flipped, and it carries the same *tycks* as the key); B `plausible_worldknowledge` (the expectation the lede overturns: medicines dominated); D `overgeneralisation` (scope deleted — "oavsett ortens storlek och vilka inrättningar som fanns där") |
| 2 | `detalj_ospecificerad` | **A** | B `surface_lexical_echo` with wrong frequency (visitation every year, not vart tredje år); C `plausible_worldknowledge` (privilege as a ban on other trade — exactly what Follinger argued in 1856 and lost); D `plausible_worldknowledge` (apprentice duty + neighbouring district) |
| 3 | `inference_slutsats` | **D** | A `reversed_causality` (trade caused the privilege); B `overgeneralisation` ("samtliga … tvingats lägga ned"); C `plausible_worldknowledge`, hedged (the farmakopé treated as a formality — the passage says the duty was met *till bokstaven*, just expensively) |
| 4 | `huvudbudskap_syfte` | **B** | A `detail_as_main` + distortion (visitation promoted to the thing that decided stock); C `detail_as_main` + fabricated quantity (leeches "en femtedel av posterna" and "säkraste inkomst" — the passage gives no share and no such claim); D `overgeneralisation` ("entydigt", "saknade betydelse" — contradicted by *Något entydigt mönster är det ändå inte fråga om*) |

Key derivation: Q1 and Q2 paraphrase single target sentences with synonym-shift
(no verbatim lift anywhere — checked sentence by sentence). Q3 requires
combining **two** passage facts in the stated direction (the privilege's
obligations followed the town, not the customer base § 2 + medicines under a
third of posts while trade goods take over half § 5). Q4 spans lede + finding +
caveats + the author's own refusal to call the pattern an explanation.

**Law 11 (no verbatim-true distractors in "bäst"-items):** every Q4 distractor
carries one identifiable flaw — A distorts a real detail into a causal claim the
text never makes, C invents a share and an income claim, D absolutises against
an explicit disclaimer. None is faithfully true.

**Hedge balance (law 10):** the key is hedged only in Q1; in Q2, Q3 and Q4 it is
the confident, specific claim. In Q1 the reversed-causality distractor A carries
the same *tycks*, and in Q3 the wrong option C is hedged while the key is not —
so "pick the qualified option" scores at chance across the unit. Key letters
spread **C, A, D, B**. The key is not the longest option in any of the four
questions (option word counts: Q1 20/17/**18**/19, Q2 **18**/21/19/17,
Q3 19/16/19/**16**, Q4 18/**15**/17/15).

## Self-blind-solve

Solved all four from the passage alone, arguing actively *for* every non-keyed
option before rejecting it.

- **Q1 = C.** A is the same content with the arrow reversed — the passage orders
  the events (district shrank *after the neighbouring town got its own apotek*,
  trade grew *during the decades thereafter*), so A is refuted by sequence, not
  by hedging. B is refuted by "knappt en tredjedel … gäller läkemedel över huvud
  taget". D is refuted by the closing line of § 5 (no such difference could be
  shown in the larger towns). Single defensible answer.
- **Q2 = A.** B fails on frequency (vart tredje år) and on inventing a journal
  duty; C is explicitly denied ("Vad han därutöver fick sälja över disken sade
  brevet ingenting om") and the 1856 ruling repeats the denial; D is unsupported
  in full. Single defensible answer.
- **Q3 = D.** Tested C hardest, since the added farmakopé paragraph makes it
  tempting (thin quantities of the last items on the list) — but the passage
  states the duty *was* met to the letter, so "a formality that could be
  disregarded" is not licensed. A inverts the direction; B overgeneralises to
  "samtliga" against four null apotek and one reversal. Single defensible answer.
- **Q4 = B.** A, C and D each fail on an identifiable flaw (above); B is the
  only option that both states the whole-text finding and preserves the text's
  own admission that the material does not explain the differences between
  towns. Single defensible answer.
