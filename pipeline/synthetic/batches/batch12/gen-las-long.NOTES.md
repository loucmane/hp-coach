# gen-las-long — authoring notes (Batch 12)

**Unit:** LÄS long, 4 questions. `family: vattentornsarkitektur-facktext-long`.
**Topic (batch-12 exclusive):** vattentornens historia och arkitektur i svenska
städer — invented county (Vitterviks län), invented towns (Silvberga, Almstorp,
Kvarnhult, Tövedal, Rönnsäter, Almsjö, Vidmark), invented researchers
(byggnadsantikvarien Solveig Rahmqvist, ingenjören Elis Wahlbeck, arkitekten
Märta Ödmann, ekonomihistorikern Gunborg Sandell) and an invented byline
(Jonatan Vestlund, arkitekturhistoriker).

## Genre and register rationale

`macro_genre: sakprosa`, `fine_genre: facktext_larobok`, `opening_move:
result-lede`. A survey-of-a-corpus facktext lets the passage carry what LÄS
difficulty actually needs: one hedged, directional, scoped finding; a hard
technical rule stated once and never repeated; a named critic with a rival
explanation; the author's own methodological caveat; and a residue of concrete
material that points nowhere. Nominal register and `-s`-passives are present
(*bestämdes*, *avgjordes*, *graderats*, *sattes upp*, *togs med*, *lämnats
öppna*). Sentence length runs 4–47 tokens (mean 20.6), so the rhythm varies the
way real prose does rather than settling into a uniform clause length.

**Anti-tidiness (law 9).** The argument does not resolve. Rahmqvist answers
Sandell (three of seven flat towns never got a railway); Sandell answers back
(two had sawmills, the third a harbour); the passage says outright that one of
them is wrong and the material cannot say which. Almstorp is a genuine
counter-example to the finding — no heights, no church tower, and still an
unrendered cylinder decided by lowest bid. Vidmark, the plainest tower in the
county, sits on the flattest ground. Four of seventeen show no difference at
all. The Tövedal expropriation, the fire brigade's hose-drying, the caretaker
flat with a bathtub but no laundry, the scratched date from September 1911, the
pump order sent to the wrong town and the 1926 wedding party on the roof are
concrete residue that serves no question.

## Frame

Title line `Det som måste stå högt` (5 words, no terminal punctuation, no
article lead). Byline last inside `passage`; glossary at the very tail, three
entries, each defining a word that actually occurs in the text (*lanternin*,
*listverk*, *tryckstegringsstation*). No title repetition at the head of the
passage.

## Trap architecture per question

Family mix: 3 detail-retrieval + 1 huvudbudskap — the corpus proportion for a
long passage (~75 % detail).

### Q1 — `enligt_texten_detalj` — key **C**
Target: the planted finding in ¶1. It is **hedged** (*tycks*), **directional**
(missing heights → costly form, never the reverse) and **scoped** (only in
towns that had nothing tall to look up at). The key paraphrases with a synonym
shift (*förefaller*, *staden i övrigt var låg*); no clause is lifted.
- **A `reversed_causality`** — the wish for a showpiece decides the placement.
  The passage makes the placement forced. Deliberately carries the same *tycks*
  as the key so hedge-spotting does not resolve the item; the Silvberga council
  minute about "det första en resande såg från järnvägen" gives it apparent
  support that concerns the ornament, not the choice of site.
- **B `plausible_worldknowledge`** — old = ornate, post-war = bare. Refuted by
  ¶3: two of the most elaborate towers are from the 1950s, one of the plainest
  from 1901.
- **D `overgeneralisation`** — drops the scope clause ("oavsett vilka andra
  höga byggnader"). Refuted by the scope itself and by Vidmark.

### Q2 — `detalj_ospecificerad` — key **A**
Target: the hydraulic rule in ¶2. Key is the **confident, specific** claim (no
hedge) — this is where the unit breaks the qualified/correct correlation.
- **B `detail_as_main` + quantity distortion** — answers with the *volume* rule
  instead of the *height* rule, and upgrades one day's consumption to two while
  inventing a derivation from population.
- **C `plausible_worldknowledge`** — a shared county-wide pressure network
  would be sound engineering, but ¶2 says each town ran its own and no
  interconnected system ever existed.
- **D `reversed_causality` on the mechanism** — makes pump capacity set the
  height. ¶2 separates the two explicitly: the pump decided how fast the tower
  filled, not how high it had to be (Kvarnhult, nine hours).

### Q3 — `detalj_ospecificerad` — key **D**
Target: Sandell's objection in ¶6. Key is again **confident and specific**.
- **A `attribution_swap`** — the 1958 widening of the façade-feature list is a
  real weakness in the text, but it is *Rahmqvist's own* stated main objection
  to herself, and she recomputes without the post-1958 entries. A solver who
  remembers the objection but not who raised it lands here. This is the hardest
  distractor in the unit and the reason the stem names Sandell explicitly: the
  flaw is identifiable, not a matter of taste.
- **B `overgeneralisation`** — inflates a methodological worry into "no
  relationship can be tested at all". Sandell does not reject the test; she
  offers a rival explanation of the same result.
- **C `plausible_worldknowledge` on a false premise** — a survivorship
  objection, standard in architectural history, but ¶8 says all seventeen
  towers still stand and two have been converted to housing. Nothing was
  demolished, so the objection has no material.

### Q4 — `huvudbudskap_syfte` — key **B**
Key spans the whole text: the shared technical task (¶2), the formal variation
(¶1, ¶3), the two irreconcilable explanations (¶6) and Rahmqvist's own verdict
that she has a pattern that survives recomputation but no explanation (¶8).

**Law 11 check — every distractor carries one identifiable flaw; none is
verbatim-true:**
- **A `detail_as_main`** — the caretaker flat in Rönnsäter and the fire
  brigade's hoses in Tövedal are single towers, introduced in the paragraph the
  passage marks as belonging to no question, and the text never ties façade
  spending to any residential use. "Främst" is the distortion.
- **C `overgeneralisation` of a quantity** — "samtliga" against the text's *two
  of seventeen*, plus an invented category (viewing platforms after
  decommissioning) that the passage does not mention for that period.
- **D `detail_as_main` with a wrong comparison and a wrong division of labour** —
  Wahlbeck did the structure on five, Ödmann the façades on six, and ¶3 opens
  by saying no engineer answered for what went on the outside. Hedged
  (*tycks*) while the key is not, which is where the unit's hedge balance is
  paid back.

## Cross-question corroboration guard

The four keys assert four different propositions:
1. the scoped placement→ornament finding;
2. the hydraulic head rule;
3. Sandell's wealth confounder;
4. the whole-text verdict (shared task, partly unresolved variation).

No option in any question states, negates or corroborates another question's
key. Q4's distractors deliberately live on axes no other question touches
(caretaker dwellings, post-decommission reuse, authorship of the façades), so
eliminating them yields nothing for Q1–Q3. The reversed-causality operation is
used twice but on unrelated targets (site choice in Q1, pump mechanism in Q2),
so "the reversal is always wrong" does not generalise into a sheet-level
shortcut. The 1958 criteria change appears as a distractor in Q3 only and is
never the subject of another item.

## Hedge balance and length tell

- Keys: Q1 hedged (*förefaller*), Q2 confident, Q3 confident, Q4 measured but
  unhedged. "Pick the qualified option" scores at chance.
- Counter-hedged distractors: Q1 A carries *tycks*; Q4 D carries *tycks*.
- M-FORM: in no question is the key the sole non-absolutized option (Q1 has no
  absolutizer anywhere; Q2 none; Q3 only C carries *endast*; Q4 only C carries
  *samtliga* while the key itself contains *alla*).
- M-TELL: key is strictly longest in 2 of 4 (Q1, Q4); in Q2 distractor C is
  longest, in Q3 the key ties with two distractors. Option-length ratios
  1.06–1.20 against the 5.25 cap.

## Self-blind-solve

Solved the four questions as a **sheet**, from the passage alone, arguing
actively for each non-keyed option before rejecting it.

- **Q1 → C.** A fails on direction (¶1 makes the site forced by missing
  heights); B fails on the 1950s/1901 sentence; D fails on the scope clause and
  on Vidmark. Only C survives. Single defensible answer.
- **Q2 → A.** B answers a different question (volume) with a wrong number; C is
  contradicted by "något sammankopplat system fanns aldrig i länet"; D is
  contradicted by the sentence separating fill time from height. Only A.
- **Q3 → D.** A is true content under the wrong name; B overshoots what Sandell
  claims; C rests on demolitions the text denies. Only D.
- **Q4 → B.** A, C and D are each single-paragraph claims with a demonstrable
  distortion; B is the only option that holds across ¶1–¶8, and it is the only
  one that accommodates both the finding and the unresolved dispute.

No question is answerable without the passage: every entity, date, count and
measurement is fictional and passage-internal, and no item turns on real
knowledge of Swedish water supply.
