# HP-Coach whole-app redesign — bake-off round 1 brief (2026-10-09)

Owner request: "a redesign of the whole app — more cutting edge and state of the art. Top tier, polished and easy to navigate." The owner looked at a reference board of 30 top-tier products and was not sure about any of them in the abstract, so round 1 shows the candidate directions **on HP-Coach's own screens** so the owner can react to real things. Round 2 extends the one or two winners to every screen.

## Who and what
- Primary user: a Swedish student with ADHD-PI aiming for a perfect högskoleprov score (2.0). Sessions are 20–60 min of focused practice; long reading passages (Swedish LÄS, English ELF).
- Pillars: one obvious next action per screen; visible progress; calm focus while reading; low friction; keyboard-first power use, but ⌘K is an accelerator, never primary navigation; everything reachable without the palette.
- UI strings Swedish. ELF passages English (mark ELF passage containers `lang="en"`).

## Shared information architecture (same in every direction)
Five destinations, always visible on every non-drill screen (desktop sidebar/rail or dock; phone bottom bar): **Idag** (home: today's plan, resume) · **Öva** (sections + Repetera) · **Provpass** · **Uppslag** (lessons/frameworks) · **Framsteg** (progress; Historik lives inside it). Account/settings is reachable from every primary screen (sidebar footer on desktop, avatar button top-right on phone) — not only from Home. A drill is focus mode: navigation recedes, with an explicit, always-visible exit ("Avsluta") and a progress indicator. Fixes known IA bugs: no screen without navigation, no dead tabs, no browser-back-only exits.

## Round 1 screens (each direction renders all of them)
1. **Idag (Home)** — realistic mid-journey state: greeting/date, exam countdown, a dominant resume card ("Fortsätt: LÄS · fråga 3 av 5"), today's plan (3 items, one primary), a projected score with the calibration caveat, top-trap pattern, recent passes. One primary action, unmistakable.
2. **LÄS reading drill, desktop + phone** — a real P5 passage + question (use the committed fixtures: pipeline/synthetic/infold/preview/sample/p5-bank-sample.json and data/explanations/p5-pilot.json — the same content the /dev/ovningstext-bakeoff fixtures use, see app/src/components/devbake/ovningstextFixtures.ts). Include the ÖVNINGSTEXT disclosure (approved copy in docs/p5-infold-design.md §3 B / Amendment 1 B, verbatim), paragraph numbers in the margin, 5 options (A–E) with keyboard hints, timer/progress, exit. Desktop: decide per direction between a resizable split view (passage left, sticky question right — Bluebook precedent) and single column; the research favours split view for long passages.
3. **Feedback + explanation** — the same question after answering wrong: verdict (glyph + word, never colour alone), correct answer, solution_path, steps (essential/detail tiers, progressive disclosure), each distractor's why_tempting / why_wrong, AND the currently-unrendered `technique` and `pitfall` fields, citing ¶ numbers. One clear "Nästa" action.
4. **Navigation chrome** — desktop sidebar/rail or dock in its expanded and collapsed/focus states; phone bottom bar; account/settings entry. Shown around screens 1 and 2.
Each at desktop 1440×900 and phone 390×844, light and dark (dark lowers chroma, never pure black). 4 directions × 4 screens × 2 widths × 2 themes.

## The four directions (each a complete, distinct design system — not recolours)
Every direction: OKLCH tokens; body text contrast ≥ 7:1, UI ≥ AA; the reading surface is always opaque; visible `:focus-visible` rings; hit targets ≥ 44 px; `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured; light is the default reading theme.

**A · Folio — editorial paper, next generation.** HP-Coach as a beautifully set book, but contemporary (not the current M3 Boksidan look reskinned). Literata (variable, opsz) for passages/headings, Inter with opsz 14–32 for UI, tabular numerals. Warm paper oklch(97% 0.012 85) / warm charcoal oklch(21% 0.01 60); one accent (ink-blue or oxblood). Flat, hairlines, structure by spacing. Motion 120–180 ms ease-out, page-like transitions, no springs. Quiet sidebar collapsing to a rail; 4–5 bottom tabs. Explanation unfolds inline like a footnote. Celebration: typographic colophon card. Risk: too quiet → one filled accent button per screen, big resume card. References: Readwise Reader, Things 3, Craft, Khan type austerity, Linear's 2026 warm greys.

**B · Instrument — precision pro tool.** Linear/Raycast-grade cockpit: fast, precise, keyboard-native. Geist + Geist Mono (UI, numbers, keycaps); passage in Atkinson Hyperlegible Next. Warm neutral greys, one electric accent (indigo or coral). Layered greys with 1 px inner strokes, keycap chips showing shortcuts (1–5 answer, J/K, E eliminate, X explain). Motion < 100 ms, optimistic. Dimmed sidebar that recedes + breadcrumb; phone bottom bar. Explanation in a right inspector panel (desktop) / sheet (phone). Progress: dense section table with sparklines. Celebration: one calm image/illustration at session end (Superhuman). Risk: density → a focus toggle hides everything but passage+question; Home stays sparse. References: Linear, Raycast, Superhuman, Notion Calendar, Vercel Geist.

**C · Spår — expressive learning path.** Guided, colourful, motivating but bounded: Material 3 Expressive shape + spring. Bricolage Grotesque display, Figtree UI and passages (18–19 px). Eight section hues at matched OKLCH lightness/chroma, tinting headers only, never behind passage text. Large radii, shape morphs on selection, soft shadows. Springs via CSS `linear()` for selection/progress; celebration tied to the real increment. Desktop: M3 expandable rail; phone bottom bar; Home as a vertical path of the day's steps. Explanation slides up as a sheet with a "varför fällan lockar" chip. Risk: overload/streak anxiety → no motion during reading, a calm toggle, forgiving streak with no loss framing, whitespace over containers (Duolingo 2026). References: Brilliant, Duolingo 2026 tabs, Speak, M3 Expressive.

**D · Lager — spatial depth, glass done right.** An opaque reading sheet floats over a soft, static, section-tinted ambient field that desaturates to neutral when reading starts. Glass (≥24 px blur, opaque fallback, reduce-transparency honoured) ONLY on navigation and controls — never text on glass except short labels. Newsreader (opsz) for passages, system UI stack (SF) with Inter fallback, Fraunces (SOFT axis) for rare display. Three z-levels (field, sheet, glass controls); View Transitions between sheets; a floating labelled dock (bottom centre on desktop, floating tab bar on phone) that may shrink on scroll but never hides or reorders. Question as a separate floating sheet beside the passage (desktop) / bottom sheet (phone); explanation stacks as a third sheet. Risk: contrast → test controls against the worst backdrop. References: Apple Liquid Glass + HIG Materials ("no glass in the content layer"), Things for OS 26, Arc/Dia; NN/g "Liquid Glass Is Cracked" as the anti-pattern list.

## Reading UX (all directions)
Measure 55–70 characters (≈60 for Swedish); body 18–20 px desktop, 17–18 px phone; line height 1.5–1.65; must survive WCAG 1.4.12 text-spacing overrides. Paragraph numbers in the margin so explanations cite "¶3". Phone: passage first, question in a sticky bottom sheet that can peek/expand while the passage scroll position is kept. Chrome hides while reading; timer can shrink to a thin bar. Optional focus aids (line/paragraph focus, answer elimination) shown as affordances.

## Craft bar — what separates top tier from template
Avoid: default shadcn greys, purple→blue gradients, emoji or mismatched-stroke icons, equal-weight cards everywhere, centred hero + 3 cards, Inter at every size without opsz, one radius on everything, generic confetti, "No data" empty states.
Do: nested radius = outer − padding, 2–3 radius tokens; optical alignment of icons/glyphs; real dashes and Swedish quotes (”…”); `tabular-nums` for scores/timers; `text-wrap: pretty` body / `balance` headings; opsz tied to size; Swedish hyphenation in narrow columns; one easing family, 2–3 durations, exits faster than entries, never block input; skeletons that match final layout; 2 px offset focus rings; 4 px baseline grid; dark mode lowers chroma rather than inverting.
