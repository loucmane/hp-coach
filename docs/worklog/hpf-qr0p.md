  - Instrument adds ⌘K, G-sequences, X, and ↑/↓ + ↵ on its Idag command list.
- The switcher's "Om …" panel describes the direction (what makes it distinct, its known risk and answer, its references) and the screen.

### Shared foundation

- **Content (fixtures).** A real P5 LÄS unit, `las-b14-002` r1 "Magasinet i Hökaryd: en handbok om socknens spannmål" — a nine-paragraph book review with four questions — from `pipeline/synthetic/infold/preview/sample/p5-bank-sample.json`, and its four reviewed explanations from `data/explanations/p5-pilot.json`. Chosen over the `/dev/ovningstext-bakeoff` unit (`las-b19-002`) because question 3 has everything the brief asks the feedback to show: essential **and detail-tier** steps, a technique, a pitfall and explicit paragraph references ("Sjätte stycket", "sjunde stycket"). The drill resumes at question 3 of 4 after two answers; Facit shows the fixture's wrong pick A — the trap the pitfall names (»förbigår« vs »i förbigående«). The approved ÖVNINGSTEXT copy (badge, note, estimate caveat) is verbatim from `docs/p5-infold-design.md` §3 B / Amendment 1 B; framework names and the trap patterns come from `app/public/frameworks/*_taxonomy.json`. The Home state (resume, plan, prognosis, traps, recent passes, week) is invented but shaped like the live Home's data and checked against the unit and `EXAM_SITTINGS` (Höstprov 26, 16 days from Friday 9 October). `r1Fixtures.test.ts` pins every copied string to its source.
- **Brief vs data.** The brief asks for "5 options (A–E)"; every P5 LÄS (and ELF) unit has **4** options, as in the real test, so all screens show A–D (keys A–D).
- **¶ citations, derived, never rewritten.** `citeParagraphs` reads ordinal references ("sjätte stycket"), verbatim quotations that occur in the passage (Swedish ”…” paired, 12+ characters) and years/names that occur in exactly one paragraph (a genitive counts as its name, so "Grimlunds" does not cite ¶1). `findQuotes` locates each verbatim quotation as a character range, so directions underline the exact sentence an explanation step quotes ("step-N") or a distractor's why_wrong quotes ("why-L"). `(steg N)` references become links to the step.
- **Drill state machine (`useDrill`).** Select → lock → feedback → next → done, eliminations, paragraph focus, cited/flashed paragraphs and sources, opened detail steps, the clock; kept in step with the switcher (answering on Läsfråga moves the switcher to Facit without a remount, so the reader's own answer stays; clicking Facit while a question is open grades it with the fixture's wrong pick). Plus `useSheet` (phone sheet peek/open/full with drag), `useHideOnScroll`, `useKeyMap` (capture phase; leaves text fields, controls' Enter/Space and command modifiers alone), `useWebFonts` (injects a direction's Google Fonts `<link>` the first time it mounts — never in `app/index.html`), `useDismiss`.
- **Shared contract.** Every direction carries the same `rd-*` test ids, so one suite drives all four; and all four use the same feedback **content order** (verdict + correct answer → why your pick tempted and why it is wrong → what to take away: pitfall + technique → solution → steps, detail collapsed → the other options, folded), so the owner compares design, not content order.
- **Isolation.** Each direction scopes its OKLCH tokens and every rule under `[data-rd='<slug>']` (`fo-`, `in-`, `sp-`, `la-` prefixes), with light and dark under `[data-theme]` on the stage — nothing touches `:root` or `index.css`. None reuses the M3 chassis (`BoksidanDesk`, `StudyDesk`, `hpc-m3-*`). Each direction is its own lazy chunk with its own CSS file.

### Process

Folio was built first and polished through three screenshot rounds before the next direction started (the brief's "one direction to polish first"). Instrument, Spår and Lager were then built in parallel by forked builders on the same kit, each with its own screenshot harness and critique log. Every direction then got an independent second reader (a fresh agent that saw only the shots and the brief: "does this look like a 2026 top-tier product, or like a template?"), and its findings were applied. Last, an independent side-by-side reader compared all four. It ruled Folio and Instrument too alike, so round 3 pushed the two apart: Folio became one sheet of paper and Instrument a grey chassis of panels. Folio got a fresh second reader after that change. A blind re-check then confirmed Folio and Instrument were apart, but found Lager borderline with Folio in dark mode and with Spår on phone, so Lager got a focused pass within the brief. A blind final check confirmed Idag was distinct everywhere but found Lager's drill still borrowing Instrument's two-pane look, so Lager got one last pass that gave its drill a layout of its own. The final sweep (`final-r5/`) is on the code after all of that. Fallback faces in every shot: the sandbox proxy refuses Google Fonts (407), so readers judged hierarchy, spacing, colour and composition, not letterforms.
